How to download a CSV statement
  • Monzo: Account → Statements → Export → CSV
  • Starling: Account → Statements → Export → CSV
  • Barclays, HSBC, Lloyds, Halifax, NatWest, Santander, Nationwide: online banking on a computer → Statements / Transactions → Export or Download → choose CSV
  • Pick at least 3 months so monthly payments show up clearly. 12 months catches yearly renewals too.
