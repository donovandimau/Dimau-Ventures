How buying works

  • Payments are taken securely by Stripe. We never see or store your card details.
  • Digital packs are delivered straight after payment by a download link, and a copy of the link goes to your email.
  • Subscriptions renew automatically and can be cancelled at any time from the app; you keep access to the end of the period you paid for.
  • Prices are shown in pounds sterling unless stated; Fiji-market listings are in Fiji dollars.

Refunds and support

  • If a download does not arrive or a pack has a fault, email us and we will fix it or refund you in full.
  • Because digital packs are delivered instantly, we cannot refund once the file has been downloaded, except where it is faulty or not as described.
  • Subscription charges taken within 14 days of first purchase are refunded on request.
  • We reply to every support email within two working days.
