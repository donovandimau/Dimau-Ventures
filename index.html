How buying works

Simple, secure and instant.

Choose

Pick a pack, a subscription or a listing. Prices are in pounds sterling unless stated; Fiji-market listings are in Fiji dollars.

Pay securely

Payments are taken by Stripe. We never see or store your card details.

Get it straight away

Digital packs arrive by download link straight after payment, and a copy of the link goes to your email.

Stay in control

Subscriptions renew automatically and can be cancelled any time from the app. You keep access to the end of the period you paid for.

Refunds

  • If a download does not arrive or a pack has a fault, email us and we will fix it or refund you in full.
  • Because digital packs are delivered instantly, we cannot refund once the file has been downloaded, except where it is faulty or not as described.
  • Subscription charges taken within 14 days of first purchase are refunded on request.

Support
